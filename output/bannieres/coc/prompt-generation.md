# Bannière COC2 — invite du 31/10/2026

Outil : génération native d’images. Illustration nouvelle ; les trois captures fournies servent
à l’analyse du médium et de la palette. Aucune illustration de référence n’est copiée ou montée
dans le panorama. Les dimensions réelles de la sortie seront mesurées après génération.

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: panoramic background banner for a tabletop game master's application header.

Theme: contemporary cinematic adventure and investigation, modern urban mystery, inviting but tense, original setting without a named franchise.

Scene: a continuous wide view along a rain-wet city canal at blue hour. Low brick and concrete city blocks and an angular steel pedestrian bridge form a clear horizontal silhouette around the middle. A few softly lit windows create restrained warm accents. The city is intact and inhabited, not abandoned or post-apocalyptic. No foreground characters. The panorama should suggest many possible contemporary adventures without committing to a specific campaign or genre of monster.

Composition: create at the largest native resolution available, preferably 3840 x 1280 pixels, so a central 4:1 crop at least 2400 x 600 can be exported without enlargement. The intended final art is a continuous 4:1 panorama. Keep all recognizable architecture and visual interest inside a very thin horizontal band at the vertical center. Upper and lower thirds must be quiet violet haze and soft shadow, not important objects. Keep the left 20 percent and right 30 percent dark, calm and low-detail for an overlaid game title and controls. Center the distinctive bridge span slightly left of the middle. At a crop only 54 pixels high, the bridge and canal-side silhouette must remain recognizable. No collage, triptych, poster or separate panels.

Lighting & palette: low-key soft directional illumination, subdued violet and deep petrol shadows with sparse muted amber reflections. Three-color harmony, restrained contrast, matte surfaces and atmospheric depth. No brilliant sun, neon signs, glowing portals or aggressive highlights. Preserve dark quiet edges.

Style: painterly digital illustration, semi-realistic graphic novel rendering, angular shapes, faceted shading, selective dark contours, crisp silhouettes, bold value grouping, violet shadows, muted teal midtones, warm amber accents, directional light, restrained rim highlights, visible brushwork, matte surfaces, cinematic depth, subtle grain, energetic pulp mood. Background-only illustration with an opaque background.

Exclusions: no text, letters, numbers, signage, logo, watermark, signature, frame, border or interface. No close-up faces, no foreground characters. Avoid photorealism, glossy 3D, anime, chibi, flat vector art, neon overload, readable text and logos. Do not reproduce an existing book cover or film still.
```
