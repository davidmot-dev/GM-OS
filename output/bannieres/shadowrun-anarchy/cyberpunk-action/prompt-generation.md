Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original opaque panoramic header illustration for a cyberpunk urban-fantasy tabletop role-playing game.
Theme: high-tech street action at night, rain, speed, corporate surveillance and rebellious energy.
Scene: a kinetic mid-distance chase across a sleek elevated roadway in a dense futuristic city. A compact armored courier vehicle accelerates through wet pavement, a small motorcycle banks sharply behind it, and two agile security drones sweep low across the lane. Tiny helmeted rider silhouette only, no recognizable faces, no foreground people. Water sprays, a restrained orange shower of sparks, slanting rain and short cyan/magenta light trails convey immediate movement. NO gothic spires, ancient towers or medieval stonework. Original setting and vehicle designs, do not copy an existing film, game cover or vehicle brand.
Composition: continuous ultra-wide 4:1 panorama, ideally native 3840x960 px or at least 2400x600. If the tool requires extra vertical canvas, keep every meaningful action motif inside the narrow central horizontal strip for a later centered 4:1 crop. Center the chase slightly left of center, around x40-50 percent and y50 percent, compact enough to remain identifiable in a 54-pixel-high centered cover header. Left 20 percent and right 30 percent dark, quiet and low detail for application text and controls. Calm upper and lower thirds; level readable cityscape, motion in the central band, no split panels.
Lighting & palette: midnight blue base, assertive petrol cyan and magenta accents, limited warm amber sparks; painted reflections and diffuse neon glow, controlled bright areas only around the central chase, dark edges, no blinding white lights.
Style: semi-realistic digital concept painting with energetic visible brushstrokes, textured broken edges, layered colored glazing, strong readable silhouettes, cinematic atmospheric depth and painterly rain. Dynamic and vivid without photorealistic camera rendering or polished 3D.
Exclusions: no text, readable letters, numbers, logos, trademarks, interface, HUD, captions, border, watermark, signature, giant foreground figures, close-up faces or gore.
